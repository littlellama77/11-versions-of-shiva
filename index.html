How to add real photos:
• Quick Upload (Right now in browser): Click “Choose Photo” on any slot below to select an image from your computer. It will immediately show up in the scrapbook polaroid frame!
• Permanent Files: You can also copy your pictures into the assets/images/ folder and update filenames in letters-data.js.
• Even without photos, elegant placeholder sketches will display automatically.
